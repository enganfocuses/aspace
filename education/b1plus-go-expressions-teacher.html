3. Translation — Russian → English

  1. When the coach saw the result, he went mad.
  2. Everyone went wild when the band came on stage.
  3. His hair has started to go grey.
  4. The pages of this book have gone yellow over the years.
  5. The sky turned gold at sunset.
  6. She went red when everyone started singing.
  7. He went deaf after years of working with loud machines.
  8. The passengers were becoming impatient because the train was delayed.
  9. This species became extinct more than a hundred years ago.
  10. Many families became homeless after the flood.
  11. She fell ill and was taken to hospital.
  12. The room fell silent when they heard the news.
  13. The noise was gradually growing louder.
  14. I have been getting tired more often lately.

Допускай другие грамматически правильные варианты, если они сохраняют исходный смысл.

5. Error correction

  1. He has known her since school.
  2. I finished my project yesterday.
  3. How long have you been waiting here?
  4. She has written three reports today.
  5. They have lived here for ten years.
  6. I'm tired because I have been working all night.
  7. Have you ever been to London?
  8. The situation has become worse since Monday. / The situation has got/gotten worse since Monday.
